Habits scorer flow

How a covered skill's output is graded before you see it, and what changes in an --auto-mode run. Drawn from the approved spec and the 15-task plan.

Level
80 by default; any whole number from 60 to 100 in ALFRED_CODE_HABITS_SCORER_LEVEL.
Rounds
A grade plus up to 3 revisions per attempt, one regrade per round at most; an inline plan gets one grade.
Off
The default. --habits-scorer-off turns it off for the session, even when the variable is 1.

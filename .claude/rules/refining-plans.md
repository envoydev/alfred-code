# Grade a draft before asking for its approval

- A spec, design or implementation plan written for the user's approval in this repo (`docs/superpowers/specs/` and `docs/superpowers/plans/` included) is graded with the `refining-plans` skill BEFORE the step that asks the user to review it, after the author's own self-review. Why: the user's bar is 9 or 10 out of 10 from an independent grade, and they should not have to ask for it (the user, 2026-10-24).
- The handoff carries the score path; a draft handed over ungraded is the failure this rule exists for.
- A user message starting with 'stop scoring' turns this rule off for the rest of the session; the handoff then says `ungraded - scoring stopped`.
